# Let Me Introduce Myself

I'm currently a Data Scientist at OneCredit, Malaysia's newest credit reporting agency and an AI-driven fintech platform.

Some of the work I've done there:
- Led the build of an AI system that translates Malaysian court decisions from Malay to English. I sized the work with SQL and evaluated LLMs: in human review, 99.5% of translations kept their meaning, against 58.7% for Google Translate.
- Designed privacy safeguards so only masked text reaches outside AI providers, using local NER models, leak tests before each release, and batch processing agreed with Legal.
- Shipped the system to production within two months (Python, Dagster, AWS): a nightly job plus a backfill of 500,000+ decisions at half the real-time cost, with masking made 1.9× faster.

Before that, I was a Data Analyst at MoneyLion, where I worked on analytics across key product funnels and partnered closely with product and engineering teams to turn data into actionable decisions. My work there focused on experimentation, data quality, and user behavior analysis, with an emphasis on finding high-impact opportunities and improving how products perform.

Some of the problems I worked on:
- Designed and analyzed A/B experiments that shaped rollout decisions, including a full rollout to 100% treatment
- Resolved a large-scale data mismatch (~100K users), improving attribution accuracy and downstream decision-making
- Improved product logic and engagement, such as the Insight Cards ranking and personalization model
- Mentored an intern on a personalized ranking model that surfaces more relevant content to users

I'm particularly interested in problems at the intersection of product, data, and user behavior, and in continuing to grow in data science and analytics.

When I'm not coding, I boulder, watch YouTube videos, or, if the weather's good, go for a jog! 🏃‍♀️
