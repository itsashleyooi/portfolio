# Let Me Introduce Myself

I'm currently a Data Analyst at MoneyLion, where I work on analytics across key product funnels and partner closely with product and engineering teams to translate data into actionable decisions.

My work focuses on experimentation, data quality, and user behavior analysis with an emphasis on identifying high-impact opportunities and improving how products perform.

Some of the problems I've worked on include:
- Designing and analyzing A/B experiments, influencing rollout decisions (including full rollout to 100% treatment)
- Resolving large-scale data mismatches (~100K users), improving attribution accuracy and downstream decision-making
- Improving product logic and engagement (e.g. Insight Cards ranking and personalization model)
- Mentoring an intern on a personalized ranking model to surface more relevant content to users

I'm particularly interested in problems at the intersection of product, data, and user behavior, and in continuing to grow toward work focused on data science and analytics.
